\#You can control the servos by navigating the screen through the buttons. There are two modes in the code, The one opened by default on the screen is designed to check the speed and sweep movement of the servos. 



\#Pressing the 'up', 'down', and 'ok' button together will change the screen output to exact pwm signal at that angle for easier calibration. When in this mode, pressing the 'ok' button will automatically set the servo to 90 degrees.
